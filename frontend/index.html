1. Guided capture

Place the printed calibration card and the baby's skin (forehead, chest or sole) both in frame, in good natural light if possible. Tap capture when both guides are filled.

2. Confirm regions

Drag on the photo to select the calibration card patch, then the skin region. Tap "Use whole photo" if you'd rather skip manual cropping (less accurate, but fine for a quick demo).

Step: drag to select the calibration card patch

3. Screening result

NeoLume is a screening aid, not a diagnostic device. A MONITOR or REFER_URGENTLY result should always be confirmed with a clinical TSB blood test.
